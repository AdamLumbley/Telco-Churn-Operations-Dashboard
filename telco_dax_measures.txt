# Telco DAX Measures

**Authorship:** AI-generated, human-prompted and reviewed for logical accuracy.

9 DAX measures powering the Telco churn and operations dashboards.

---

## Revenue Churn Rate %
Percentage of total MRR lost to churned customers.

Revenue Churn Rate % = 
DIVIDE(
    CALCULATE(SUM('Fact_Charges'[Monthly Charges]), 'Fact_Charges'[Churn] = "Yes"),
    [MRR]
)

---

## Customer Churn Rate %
Percentage of customers who churned, out of all customers.

Customer Churn Rate % = 
DIVIDE(
    CALCULATE(COUNTROWS('Fact_Charges'), 'Fact_Charges'[Churn] = "Yes"),
    COUNTROWS('Fact_Charges'),
    0
)

---

## MRR
Total monthly recurring revenue across all customers, regardless of churn status.

MRR = 
CALCULATE(
    SUM('Fact_Charges'[Monthly Charges]),
    ALL('Fact_Charges'[Churn])
)

---

## Churned MRR
Monthly recurring revenue lost to churned customers.

Churned MRR = 
CALCULATE(
    SUM('Fact_Charges'[Monthly Charges]),
    'Fact_Charges'[Churn] = "Yes"
)

---

## Retained MRR
Monthly recurring revenue held by customers who have not churned.

Retained MRR = 
CALCULATE(
    SUM('Fact_Charges'[Monthly Charges]),
    'Fact_Charges'[Churn] = "No"
)

---

## ARPU
Average revenue per user — total revenue divided by total customers.

ARPU = DIVIDE([Total Revenue], [Total Customers], 0)

---

## Total Churn
Count of churned customer records.

Total Churn = CALCULATE(COUNTROWS('Fact_Charges'), 'Fact_Charges'[Churn] = "Yes")

---

## Total Customers
Count of distinct customers.

Total Customers = COUNTROWS('Dim_Customer')

---

## Total Revenue
Sum of monthly charges across all customer records.

Total Revenue = SUM(Fact_Charges[Monthly Charges])
